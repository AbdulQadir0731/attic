Support

We're here to help.

Contact us by opening a request at github.com/AbdulQadir0731/attic/issues. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, the destination you use (Google Drive, Dropbox or a folder) and what you saw on screen. Please don't send photos or passwords.

Freeing up space

I removed items but my free space didn't go up

Photos you remove go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app and stay there for 30 days, still using space. To get the space back now, open Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted, then tap Select › Delete All. Only do this once you're happy the items are in your archive. Attic shows a Verified badge for each one.

Why does iOS ask me before removing items?

iOS requires your confirmation every time an app removes items from your photo library. Attic can't skip that prompt. If you tap Don't Allow, nothing changes: your items stay on your iPhone and remain Verified, ready for later.

An item says "Changed since copied"

You edited that photo or video after Attic copied it, so the archived copy no longer matches. Attic won't remove it. Archive it again to save the current version.

Copying stopped when I left the app

iOS doesn't let apps run long uploads unattended in the background, so copying works best with Attic open. The screen stays on while it runs. If you leave, Attic picks up where it stopped the next time you open it. Items that were half-uploaded start that one file again.

Destinations

Attic says I need to reconnect Google Drive or Dropbox

This happens if you changed your password, revoked Attic's access, or the sign-in expired. Go to Attic › Settings › Destinations, tap the destination, and choose Reconnect. Sign in with the same account you used before so Attic can find your existing archive.

My external drive or network folder isn't found

Because of an iOS limitation, access to an external USB-C drive or network share can stop working after it's unplugged or disconnected. Connect the drive, then go to Settings › Destinations, tap the folder destination and choose Pick Folder Again. Select the same Attic folder you used before.

My iCloud Drive items stay on "Waiting for upload"

Attic only marks an iCloud Drive copy as Verified once iOS reports the file has finished uploading to iCloud. Make sure you're online, iCloud Drive is turned on, and your iCloud storage isn't full.

My Google Drive or Dropbox is full

Attic stops the batch and tells you which items didn't copy. Nothing is removed from your iPhone. Free up space in that account, or add a different destination, then try again.

Finding and restoring

How do I restore an original to my iPhone?

Open the Attic tab, find the item (search works too), tap it, and choose Restore to iPhone. Attic downloads the original, checks it against the checksum recorded when it was archived, and adds it back to Photos with its date, location and favourite status. If you had a lite copy, Attic replaces it. Restore is always free.

I have a new iPhone and my Attic tab is empty

Connect the same destination, then go to Settings › Rebuild catalog. Attic reads the manifests it saved alongside your files and rebuilds your catalog.

What do lite copies keep?

A lite copy keeps the date, location, favourite status and album membership. People tags, Memories links and captions don't carry over. Restore the original to get everything back exactly as archived.

Purchases

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Attic Pro and tap Cancel Subscription, or use Attic › Settings › Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged. Your archive stays searchable and restorable after you cancel.

What is the Trip Pass?

The Trip Pass gives you every Pro feature for 7 days from the moment you buy it, for a single payment. It doesn't renew. Anything you archive during those 7 days stays in your archive afterwards, and restoring it is free.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Go to Attic › Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, contact us with the date of purchase. Please don't include payment details.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
