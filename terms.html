2. What Attic does

Attic copies photos and videos from your iPhone to storage you connect (Google Drive, Dropbox, or a folder you pick) and checks each copy against the original using checksums before offering to remove it from your iPhone. It can also create smaller "lite" copies in your photo library and restore originals from your archive.

iOS asks for your confirmation before any item is removed from your photo library, and removed items stay in Photos' Recently Deleted album for up to 30 days.
