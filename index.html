About Attic

What Attic is for

Attic is an iPhone app that frees up storage on your iPhone. It archives the older photos and videos you choose to storage you own (Google Drive, Dropbox, or a drive or folder you pick), proves each copy is intact, and only then lets you remove the original from your phone. You can search the archive and restore any original back to Photos at any time.

How Attic uses your Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Attic asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see just the files and folders Attic itself creates, plus your Google account email address, which Attic shows so you know which account is connected. Attic uses that access to upload the photos and videos you choose, check their checksums, keep a small manifest of your archive, and download an original when you restore it. It can't see any other files in your Drive.

Your Google account data stays on your iPhone and in your own Drive: there is no Attic server, and Attic never sells, shares or transfers Google user data. Attic's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the Attic Privacy Policy.

Who makes Attic

Attic is made by Abdul Qadir, an independent iOS developer. It's available on the App Store for iPhone with iOS 15 or later, free to start with optional Attic Pro plans.

Your storage, your files

Attic saves plain photo and video files in ordinary folders, named by date. You can open them without Attic, and there's no Attic server in between.

Google Drive

Dropbox

Attic works inside its own App folder and can't reach anything else in your Dropbox.

Your own drive or folder

A USB-C drive, a network share, or iCloud Drive, all picked through the Files app.

Honest about iOS

What to expect, up front.

iOS protects your photo library, and that shapes how any app like Attic works. We'd rather tell you now.

  • iOS asks before anything is deleted. You'll see a system prompt for each batch you remove. Attic can't skip it, and wouldn't want to.
  • Recently Deleted keeps items for 30 days. Space comes back once they leave that album, or sooner if you empty it in Photos.
  • Copying runs while Attic is open. iOS doesn't allow long uploads unattended in the background, so Attic keeps your screen on and picks up where it stopped.
  • Lite copies keep date, location, favourites and albums. People tags, Memories links and captions don't carry over. The full original in your archive is untouched.
  • External drives may need to be picked again after they're unplugged, because of an iOS limitation.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can Attic see everything in my Google Drive or Dropbox?

No. With Google Drive, Attic only has access to the files and folders it creates. With Dropbox, it works inside its own App folder. Your other files stay out of reach.

Do my photos go through your servers?

No. There is no Attic server. Files travel directly from your iPhone to the storage you connect. We don't receive your photos, your account details, or any usage data.

What does "Verified" actually mean?

After uploading, Attic asks your storage for the file's checksum (or reads the file back) and compares it with the original on your iPhone. Only an exact match is marked Verified, and only Verified items can be removed from your phone.

Why didn't my free space go up straight away?

Removed photos and videos go to Recently Deleted in Photos for 30 days. The space returns when they leave that album, or right away if you empty it.

Can I open my archive without Attic?

Yes. Your files are ordinary photos and videos in folders named by year and month, so any computer or app that reads your storage can open them.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing you've archived is affected. Search and restore stay free forever. You'll only need Pro to archive beyond the free 2 GB or to use lite copies and the mirror.

I got a new iPhone. Is my catalog gone?

No. Every batch writes a small manifest next to your files, so Attic can rebuild its catalog from your storage on a new phone. Use Settings › Rebuild catalog.
